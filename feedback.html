Frequently asked questions

How much does delivery cost?

Standard delivery is LKR 500. Orders worth LKR 10,000 or more receive free delivery.

How can I pay?

You can choose cash on delivery or card on delivery during checkout.

Can I change an order after placing it?

Contact Toy Haven as soon as possible with your order number and we will help if the order has not been dispatched.

Does the website save my cart?

Yes. Your cart and wishlist are stored in your current browser so you can return to them later.
